Hire Spacefleet's finest to staff your spaceship. At the end, you will choose your First Officer.
Here are the available positions:
First Officer;Maintains discipline among the crew.
Security Chief;Neutralizes threats.
Pilot;Sets course. Presses the "Go" button.
Chief Engineer;Habitual miracle worker. Beams you up.
Chief Medical Officer;Handy with a hypospray and a dermal regenerator.
Science Officer;Scans stuff. Says "Fascinating!"
Diplomat;Negotiates and spreads goodwill to alien races.
Bartender;Listens and supplies the prune juice.
Redshirt;I hope you're not too attached to this character....
