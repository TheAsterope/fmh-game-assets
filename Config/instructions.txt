Navigation:
1. Restart a game or return to the main menu by using buttons in the top-left.They will always be visible.
2. Sometimes, you can undo your moves by using the button in the top-right.
3. Sometimes, informational text will appear in the middle of that top navigation pane, so watch out for that.
4. When you middle-click pictures, it will open up the character's URL.

Settings:
1. Filter multiple tags by separating them with a comma. e.g.real, actress, musician
2. If you delete your cookies and temporary internet files, you may lose any saved settings.
3. If you use "Incognito Mode" or "Private Browsing", This game will not save any settings to your web browser.
4. You might be able to break certain games by having too few active characters or actions.

Games:
1. The guessing game gives you 10 seconds to start, decrements by 0.5 seconds per correct answer until a minimum of 2.0 seconds.
2. Sudoku is fun, isn't it? I'm pretty happy with that one.
3. In the prison break game, you must start at the top row and end on the bottom row. Choices are limited to follow that constraint.
4. In the encounters game, you will likely run out of points if you do not pass.

Story:
1. A wizard did it.
2. When choosing a character, you can pick them from any point in their timeline, as long as they are over the age of 18.
3. The default actions of "Fuck", "Hire" and "Marry" are meant to be "strictly sexual", "professional and sexual" and "romantic and sexual" respectively.

Thanks for playing!
